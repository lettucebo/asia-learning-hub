RULES
G1: Every check with Mandatory = Yes must have Status = Complete
and its evidence approved before a go recommendation.
G2: Any mandatory Blocked or In progress check makes the current
recommendation No-go. Only the Commercial Lead can record the final
launch decision after evidence is checked. An AI draft cannot approve it.
G3: Optional work does not block the gate but must be reported separately.
G4: Overdue means Status is not Complete and DueAt is strictly earlier
than the fixed review time. A deadline later today is not yet overdue.
G5: In this fixed sample, a Complete row's evidence summary records
approved completion. Other statuses do not imply sign-off, even where an
evidence ID exists. Evidence IDs identify records, not approvals by themselves.
G6: Percent complete = completed checks / total checks. Calculate
mandatory completion separately; neither percentage overrides G1 or G2.
