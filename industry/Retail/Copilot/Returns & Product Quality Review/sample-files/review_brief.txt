JUNIPER LANE RETAIL - RETURNS DECISION BRIEF
Synthetic demonstration. Reference date: 13 November 2026, end of day.

DATA DEFINITIONS
Sales cohort: units sold from 1 to 14 October 2026, inclusive.
Observed through: 13 November 2026. Every sale has at least 30 calendar
days of observation after the sale date. This is a fixed observation
cutoff, not a claim that every shopper has the same return entitlement.
ReturnedUnits counts distinct originally sold units returned once by
the cutoff; exclude cancelled orders, exchanges, duplicate returns,
and units from other sales cohorts. Each unit belongs to one SKU/channel.
SoldUnits is gross completed units for the cohort before these returns.
RefundCU is the actual merchandise refund amount for these units,
excluding shipping, tax, recovery, processing cost, and lost margin.
All units of each SKU in this sample were sold at the same price.
PrimaryReportedReason is the dominant reported reason for that row,
not a reason assigned to every returned unit. Reason-level counts are
not supplied and must not be invented.
Do not average row rates; aggregate numerators and denominators first.
All financial amounts are fictional currency units (CU).

OBSERVATIONS - NOT CONFIRMED CAUSES
O-01: Digital Merchandising reports that the online jacket size guide
was last reviewed in July. Store colleagues say shoppers often try the
jacket on in store. Neither observation proves why customers returned it.
O-02: Quality Desk received three leaking-lid comments mentioning bottle
batch B7. Batch-level sales and return counts, inspection findings,
and a comparison batch are missing.
O-03: Fulfilment reports that online mug packaging changed on 5 October.
Order-level packaging version, damage photos, and carrier handling
records are missing. Channel mix alone cannot isolate a packaging effect.

DECISION RULES
Prioritize investigations by measured exposure and evidence gaps.
Propose owner roles, not invented people or agreed commitments.
Do not promise savings from refunds: recovery and intervention cost are unknown.
Do not recommend a supplier chargeback, recall, or withdrawal as approved.
Any real product-safety concern must go to the retailer's accountable team.
The demonstration is not a product safety or legal assessment.
