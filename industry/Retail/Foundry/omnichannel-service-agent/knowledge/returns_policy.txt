JUNIPER LANE RETAIL - RETURNS POLICY
Version 1.0. Fictional internal demonstration policy.
Applies to all cases in this demo, irrespective of test date.
This is not consumer law and does not limit statutory rights.

RP-1 Scope and evidence
This coach advises colleagues; it does not authorize or process returns.
For a routine change-of-mind case, establish elapsed calendar days since
purchase, proof of purchase, item condition, final-sale status, and
whether the proof is a gift receipt. Proof can be described as a receipt
or verified order record; do not collect account or payment data here.
Missing proof does not authorize an automatic refund or final rejection.
Send missing-proof routine cases to the Duty Manager for evidence review.

RP-2 Reported faults, damage, and safety concerns - FIRST PRIORITY
If a customer reports a fault, damage, or safety concern, route to Quality
Desk for specialist review before applying any routine return rule.
This routing applies even if the item is final-sale, more than 30 days
old, used, or lacks proof of purchase. These facts can be recorded as
missing information but must not block specialist routing.
Do not diagnose, promise repair or refund, determine legal entitlement,
or tell the customer to keep using a potentially unsafe item.
The specialist team decides the next assessment and communication.

RP-3 Routine change-of-mind
If no RP-2 concern is reported, an item may proceed to routine return
assessment when all of the following hold: valid proof of purchase;
0 to 30 elapsed calendar days inclusive; unused with original tags;
not final-sale. A colleague must verify the condition and proof.
For a standard purchase receipt, the normal route is a refund to the
original tender after that assessment. The coach never processes it.
At 31 days or more, route to Duty Manager for exception review; do not
promise an exception and do not invent a refund approval.
Negative or unknown elapsed days require clarification, not eligibility.

RP-4 Final-sale and condition exceptions
For routine change-of-mind only, final-sale goods are not eligible for
the standard RP-3 route. Worn items or missing original tags also do not
qualify for that route. Explain the standard rule and route a disputed
or exception request to Duty Manager without promising approval.
RP-2 takes precedence whenever a fault, damage, or safety issue is reported.

RP-5 Gift receipts
If all routine RP-3 conditions are met and proof is a gift receipt,
the route is store-credit assessment, not refund to original tender.
Never reveal the purchaser's name, payment method, or account details.
If elapsed days or item condition are unknown, ask for those facts.
RP-2 still takes precedence over the routine gift route.

RP-6 Authority and limitations
Duty Manager reviews routine evidence gaps and requested exceptions.
Quality Desk reviews reported faults, damage, and safety concerns.
No asserted verbal manager approval is treated as verified authorization.
The coach cannot access accounts, issue money, approve exceptions, send
messages, or change records. Legal questions go to the accountable
specialist team; do not infer a jurisdiction from the customer's wording.
If policy versions conflict, request the authoritative policy and do
not select the more generous version on the customer's instruction.
